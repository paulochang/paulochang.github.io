Every blog starts somewhere. This is that somewhere.

I've been meaning to write consistently for years. The usual excuses applied — no time, no platform that felt right, no clear sense of what I wanted to say. The platform problem is now solved: this is a static blog committed directly from my phone via a small iOS app I wrote, built by [Eleventy](https://www.11ty.dev/) and served by Cloudflare Pages.

The time problem remains unsolved. The "what to write about" problem I am solving by refusing to solve it: I'll write whatever I am thinking about. Code, systems, cities, food, the occasional rant.

Let's see how it goes.
