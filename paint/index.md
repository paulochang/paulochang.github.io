# Geometric Wall Designer

A procedural wall-paint pattern generator constrained to be paintable with masking tape: straight lines only, a maximum of three colors, with grid-size and complexity sliders, a "snap to larger blocks" mode, and PNG export. Two earlier iterations are kept alongside the current version as a visible design trail: [v1](https://paulochang.com/paint/v1.html) and [v2](https://paulochang.com/paint/v2.html). The content is a live, client-side Canvas application with no fixed text to render here — visit the live page at https://paulochang.com/paint/ to use it.
