        Levels

        Priority tiers

        P2

        P3
        Nice to have, or specific to one tool.

        How it’s researched

        Each tool has a long-form profile in my research notes, checked against the vendor’s documentation and, where the docs are vague, against the tool itself. Every cell cites a vendor URL or the section of those notes it came from, such as tools/cursor.md §7. In the notes, “KB” means my research notes and “hub” means their summary matrix.

        If something is undocumented or the sources disagree, it stays unverified. I don’t guess. A tool that runs in several places, such as a CLI, an IDE and the cloud, is marked partial when only some of them have a feature, and the note names which.

        The page is regenerated from the notes after each review, so the date at the top is the date of the last review, not the last deploy.
