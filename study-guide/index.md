# CCAR-F Study Guide

An offline study guide for the Claude Certified Architect (Foundations) exam. It has 14 prep pages, an interactive drill with a 134-question bank, 36 recall cards, confidence ratings and a timed mock exam, and a 16-chapter companion book. Everything is compiled into one HTML file with a collapsible sidebar and light and dark themes. It has no dependencies and opens straight from disk, without a server. Drill progress is kept in your browser's localStorage.

Read it at https://paulochang.com/study-guide/.
